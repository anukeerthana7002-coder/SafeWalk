About SafeWalk

SafeWalk started from a simple idea: nobody should walk home alone, even when they are alone. We built a calm, colourful tool that keeps the people who care about you in the loop.

No feeds, no noise. Plan a route, keep trusted contact details on this device and prepare an SOS message when needed.

How does live location work?

This prototype can request your browser location and use it to prepare a map link. It does not send your location to other people in real time. Cross-device sharing needs a secure backend.

Who can see me?

This version does not broadcast your location to other users. Browser location access is optional and can be switched off.

What happens when I press SOS?

SafeWalk attempts to prepare a text message containing a map link. You must send the message yourself. The call button opens your device dialler; you must place the call yourself. Do not rely on this prototype as an emergency service.

What does “verified” mean?

This prototype does not verify identity or phone ownership. A name and phone number entered here are stored locally in this browser only.
